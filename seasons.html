SEASONS OF THE SOUL • 01

Autumn

All good things come to a bitter end.
The trees shed their precious leaves,
the birds fly for distant miles
in a desperate search
for a home of comfort.

The fearsome bear yields,
for it knows the times to come are cruel.
The largest and smallest of creatures
bend beneath
the coming cruelty.

Yet the earth remains still warm,
though not as it was before the beginning.
It is not the end—
only a season
deceiving the blinded.

The human heart and soul,
blinded by love,
move through the seasons:
the calm before the storm,
the pain to come,
distant yet real.

Perhaps it is because of the ones we love,
or the ones we hate.
But in the end,
are we not all
mere men among men?

Do they not feel what we feel?
Are we not all human?
Why should we hurt each other
when we know
we carry the same fragile heart?

It is the human heart—
the false hope we give ourselves,
how pathetically human.

We tell ourselves lies.
We choose to remain blind.
Yet somewhere within,
we all know what is to come.

And still,
we hope.

The human heart—
beautifully naive.
